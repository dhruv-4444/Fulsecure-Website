Frequently asked

What people usually want to know before writing in.

What does it cost to use a broker?

Nothing extra on top of the premium. A broker's remuneration comes from the insurer as part of the premium you'd pay regardless of how you bought the policy — comparing options across insurers doesn't add a separate fee for you.

Which insurers do you place cover with?

We work across India's general and life insurers based on fit for the specific risk. The right carrier for a motor fleet isn't necessarily the right one for a family health plan, so we confirm options once we understand what's being insured.

How do I start?

Write in through the contact page with what you're looking to insure. We'll come back with a short set of questions to size the risk, then placement options — not a single pre-packaged quote.
