Claims Advocacy

The point a broker's work actually gets tested.

A policy is only as good as what happens when you need to use it. We stay on the file from the moment a claim is intimated through to settlement.

Report a Claim / Talk to a Broker

How a claim moves

The same path whether it's a motor claim or a corporate property loss.

01

Intimation

We notify the insurer promptly and confirm the claim reference and timelines.

02

Documentation

We help assemble the evidence an insurer actually asks for — bills, reports, photographs, estimates.

03

Follow-Up

Regular check-ins with the insurer so the file doesn't go quiet while a survey or approval is pending.

04

Settlement Advocacy

If an offer looks short against the policy terms, we raise it with the insurer on your behalf before you accept.

Illustrative example

A motor claim after an accident often stalls on a surveyor's assessment or a missing document. Rather than leaving that to the policyholder to chase, we confirm what the insurer still needs, follow up on the surveyor's timeline, and flag it if the file sits longer than the insurer's own service standard allows.

What this means in practice

01

One point of contact

You deal with us, not a rotating insurer call-centre queue.

02

No separate fee

Claims support is part of the broking relationship — it isn't billed as an extra service.

03

Escalation when a file stalls

If a claim is taking longer than the insurer's own timelines allow, we escalate it directly.

04

Works for existing policies too

You don't need to have bought the policy through us for us to help with an active claim.
