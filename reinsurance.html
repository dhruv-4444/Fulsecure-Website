Reinsurance

Risk transfer above a single insurer's retention.

For exposures a direct insurer won't carry alone, we place treaty and facultative reinsurance and advise on structuring the risk before it goes to market.

What this covers

Placement and advisory for larger corporate and institutional exposures.

01

Treaty Placement

Standing arrangements that automatically reinsure a defined block of an insurer's portfolio.

02

Facultative Placement

Reinsurance negotiated risk-by-risk for exposures too large or specialised for a treaty.

03

Capacity Sourcing

Identifying reinsurers with appetite and capacity for a specific class or geography of risk.

04

Risk Advisory

Structuring retention, layers and terms before a risk is taken to the reinsurance market.

Where it sits

Reinsurance picks up where an insurer's own retention stops.

An insurer only carries a loss up to a level it's comfortable retaining. Above that, the exposure is passed on — as a treaty covering a whole portfolio, or facultative, negotiated for one specific risk.

For exposures that sit above standard retention, write in directly.
